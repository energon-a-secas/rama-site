          Getting around

          Rama draws an org from one document: people, who each reports to, and anything else you want on a profile. Click anyone to stand on their card. Their manager sits above them, their peers beside them, and everyone's reports underneath.

          ↑
          Their manager
          ↓

          ? opens the shortcut sheet, where every single-key shortcut can be turned off or moved to another key.

          Your card. Rama starts on you when it knows who you are: a ?me= in the link, the card you picked with Me, or the email on your Neorgon account. Otherwise it starts at the top.

          The document. YAML or JSON, with manager: for reporting lines and any extra key you like shown under Details. The schema, with every key, is in llms.txt. CSV exports from an HR tool import too.

          Other tools. Hand off opens the same people in Floorplan, as rooms, or in Reparto, as a team's sprint capacity, through a link. Nothing is uploaded.
