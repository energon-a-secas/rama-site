# Rama: product one-pager

**What it is:** Navigate your organization, starting with you. An org chart drawn from one
YAML, JSON or CSV document, centred on one person at a time.

**Who it is for:** someone new to a company, or new to a part of it, who needs to know who their
manager's manager is, who sits beside them and who to ask about Postgres. Second, the
engineering manager who keeps the org document and wants the same team in Floorplan and Reparto
without typing it three times.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** a laptop on a desk in the first week of a new job, a video call about to start,
and a name on the invite nobody has explained yet. Dark, calm, one glance to place a person.

**What done looks like:** the visitor knows where that person sits, who they report to, what
they know and what time it is where they are, and can hand the team to the next tool in one click.
